Lionel Messi

Greatest of all time

Lionel Messi is an Argentine professional footballer, widely considered one of the greatest ever, known for his dribbling, passing, and prolific scoring as a forward for Inter Miami and the Argentina national team, holding records like eight Ballon d'Or awards and leading Argentina to a 2022 World Cup victory, after a legendary career at FC Barcelona and a stint at PSG. Born in 1987, he overcame a growth hormone deficiency with Barcelona's help, rising from a child prodigy to a decorated global icon, winning numerous titles with club and country.
